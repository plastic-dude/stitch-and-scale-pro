## Fix
**Export PDF metadata hardcoded to English** (localization-gap class):
- `src/lib/pdf/renderer.ts` emitted `<html lang="en">` and `locale:en` in the provenance footer for every export, regardless of the document's actual locale (de/fr/es/pt exports lied to screen readers and print pipelines).
- `templateId` from `RenderContext` was never passed to `renderProvenanceFooter` — footer always reported `template:stitch-and-scale-default`.
- Fix: `renderDocument` clamps `locale` via a new exported `isValidLanguageCode` validator (`src/lib/i18n.ts`) and emits `<html lang>` + provenance `locale` + `template` from the clamped value. Pure output-metadata change; zero calculation semantics touched.
