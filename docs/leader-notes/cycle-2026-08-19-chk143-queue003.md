## What was worked

QUEUE-003: the last uncovered user-facing English layer — narrative footers ("benchmarks baked in" style) on 7 Tier-2 lab cards plus ~95 `<Field hint>` strings across 18 component files. This closes the Tier 2 localization surface; Tier 1 narratives remain as QUEUE-004.

## Scope measured

297 unique string keys across 18 files were inventoried (`docs/queue` notes + `q003_keys_hand.py` scaffold). 24 scaffold artifacts (`{copy.xxx}` references that are already dynamic) were excluded, leaving **273 real strings** — all hand-translated in **de / fr / es / pt** (tone: informal German `du`/`dein`, French formal). Numbers, currency ranges, and brand names kept verbatim.

## Implementation

- **11 new copy modules** under `src/lib/` (pod-patterns, podcast-affiliate, pricing-psychology, release-timing, retreat-teaching, show-roi, testknit-slot, wholesale-pricelist, workshop-teaching, yarn-licensing, video-social): typed `XCopy` interface, typed `en` const, locale consts spread from `en`, 5-locale `X_COPY` record export. Keys containing `%`, `$`, en-dashes, or leading digits are normalized to legal TS identifiers (e.g. `55%ForPatreon` → `k55PctForPatreon`); components use member access.
- **7 seamed modules** extended (channel-migration, collab-deal-math, collab-evaluator, convention-booth, intl-pricing, marketplace-takerate, pattern-club): new keys added to interface (quoted where unsafe) and all five locale consts with bracket notation in components, preserving legacy raw-key style.
- **All 18 components wired**: `useSettings()` + copy lookup + `label=`/`hint=`/`description=` JSX expressions. Special fixes: `pattern-club-card.tsx` pre-existing bug of literal `label="{copy.xxx}"` strings (9 occurrences, club + magazine tabs) converted to expressions; `show-roi-lab-card.tsx` local `ProductRowEditor` helper gained a `copyText` prop; `wholesale-pricelist` and `testknit-slot` insertions relocated out of local helper functions; duplicate insert cleanup in show-roi.
- **77 regression tests** (`src/lib/__tests__/*-copy.test.ts`, 7 checks per new module: all-locale coverage, DE spot check, no English leftovers in non-EN maps, unknown-code fallback).
- Incidental fixes: yarn-licensing `royalty%` had an English leftover in `de`/`pt` ("Royalty %") → "Tantiemen %"/"Tantieme %"; `0NoneNickiS` (pattern-club trial-length hint) had a French fragment "un mois gratuit" verified intentional after review of Nicki's cross-reference.
