## Reproduction
Root cause confirmed in `src/pages/landing.tsx`: the capability grid used Framer Motion `whileInView` with `initial={{opacity:0}}`. Content stayed invisible until scrolled into view, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` framer-motion freezes at the initial state, making the cards **permanently invisible** for reduced-motion users. The hero was unaffected (`initial→animate`).

## Fix (commit `6e45368`)
Capability cards now use `animate` (play once on mount) — visible on first paint, deterministic, reduced-motion-proof. Whole-tree grep confirmed no other `whileInView` opacity-0 pairs exist in `src`. 3 regression tests in new `src/pages/landing-visibility.test.tsx` (defect-signature guard, single-source catalogue, 5-locale count parity).
