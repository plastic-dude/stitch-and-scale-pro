Yarn shopping research findings (Aug 21, 2026)

1. Lion Brand yarn-quantity guide: for medium-weight yarn, estimated yardage is 200–225 yards for a hat, 275–375 yards for a scarf, 1,125–1,625 yards for an adult sweater, 1,000–1,125 yards for a baby blanket, and 2,250–3,125 yards for an afghan. Lion Brand states the figures are estimates, not exact requirements. Source: https://www.lionbrand.com/pages/how-much-yarn-do-i-need

2. Craft Yarn Council page: a 2014 tracking study of more than 3,100 knitters and crocheters reported that 87% buy yarn every one to three months; 49% buy at least once a month; 11% buy weekly. Primary sources were specialty craft chains (51%), local yarn shops (14%), mass merchandisers (14%), and the Internet (10%). Source: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/know.html

3. Current representative prices found in prior recheck: standard budget acrylic around $4.18 for Red Heart Super Saver 7 oz/364 yards at Walmart; jumbo acrylic around $7.48–$8.27 for 14 oz/744 yards; Walmart listings showed Cotton Sprout DK at $7.49 and Basix Chenille at $6.99; Knit Picks wool listings around $4.99–$6.99; Michaels cotton and wool examples were around $2.24 and $4.89 on promotion. Prices vary by sale and seller.
