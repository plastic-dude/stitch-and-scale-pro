
## Competitor pricing benchmark

Stitchmastery official FAQ: https://stitchmastery.com/frequently-asked-questions/ . It offers a free demo mode and a $95 USD activation key with lifetime access on two computers; future upgrades may cost extra. This demonstrates that at least one adjacent knitting-design tool uses a low-friction trial plus one-time purchase rather than a mandatory subscription.

Earlier research also found Payhip at $0/month plus 5% transaction fee, $29/month plus 2%, or $99/month with no Payhip transaction fee (payment processing separate), and Etsy with $0.20 listing fees, 6.5% transaction fees, variable processing, and possible offsite-ad fees. These are direct marketplace benchmarks and not recommendations for Stitch & Scale.

Implication: for budget-sensitive hobbyist mothers, a free tier plus pay-per-export or a low annual plan is more aligned with irregular project-based use than a high mandatory monthly subscription. For active pattern designers, a recurring plan or professional lifetime purchase can be justified only after measurable time and error reduction.

## Pricing sensitivity model

Illustrative hobbyist plans:

- Pay-per-export at $7.99 costs $7.99 for one project, $23.97 for three, $47.94 for six, and $95.88 for twelve projects per year.
- A $39 annual hobbyist plan costs $39 for one, three, six, or twelve projects, equivalent to $39, $13, $6.50, or $3.25 per project respectively.
- A $4.99 monthly plan costs $59.88/year, which is more expensive than the annual plan for every use level but can be appropriate for users who prefer a low monthly commitment.

Illustrative creator plan at $19/month would produce $950 MRR / $11,400 ARR at 50 paid users, $1,900 MRR / $22,800 ARR at 100 users, $4,750 MRR / $57,000 ARR at 250 users, and $9,500 MRR / $114,000 ARR at 500 users. These are gross revenue scenarios before payment fees, support, infrastructure, taxes, and refunds.

Implication: the hobbyist offer should not force a $19/month plan. Pay-per-export and a low annual pass give mothers control over spending and match irregular project behavior. A separate professional creator plan can support recurring revenue from active pattern publishers.
