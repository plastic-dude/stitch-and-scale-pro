# Brand Voice Brief — Stitch & Scale

*Prepared from public profile research, 15 Aug 2026. Living document — update as the founder's voice sharpens.*

## Who the founder is

The public face is David Mokwunye, a knitwear designer who is also a web developer — someone who wears two hats that rarely meet in one person. His public self-presentation comes from two artifacts on his profile: a terminal-style cover poster (`del limits.txt`, `breakthrough.exe`, `System status: UNSTOPPABLE`, "THIS USER IS GOING TO CHANGE THE WORLD") and a profile photo of an alien standing among humans in a crowded street. Together they say something coherent: a builder with defiant ambition who sees himself as an outsider quietly noticing what the crowd cannot — stated with a wink rather than a shout.

## The voice

| Dimension | Yes | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register | First person ("I built this because…") | Corporate "we streamline your workflow" |
| Rhythm | Short declarative lines; craft truth before tech truth | Marketing-speak, exclamation points as crutch |
| Metaphors | Terminals, code, systems — sparingly, as flavor | Jargon (ROI, synergy, leverage, empower) |
| Emotion | Quiet confidence + outsider wit + genuine love of the craft | Hype, urgency gimmicks, countdown pressure |
| Credibility | Numbers and real product ("75 labs, 1,600 tests") | Vague superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing") |

The one-line brand tension that makes the copy work: **a hacker's hands in the yarn world.** The copy should always feel like a craftsperson who can code, not a coder who knits.

## The tester-first funnel (current strategy)

The site's single job right now is to recruit **founding testers** — knitwear designers who will use the demo, say what they'd pay, and come back at launch. No paid spend until the early-access list proves demand. Everything on the page should point to one of two actions: "try the demo" (instant, no signup) or "join the founding testers" (email). The founding-tester ask is framed as membership of a small first cohort whose feedback shapes pricing and features, not as a waitlist.

## Copy seeds (approved direction)

Hero direction: craft truth first — e.g. "You can knit anything. But can you price it?" / "The pattern is only half the job. The math is the rest." Demo proof: "75 business labs. 1,600 tests. Free, no signup, your patterns stay on your machine." Founding-tester CTA: "Founding testers — the first cohort shapes what this becomes. No spam, no fee, just honest answers."

## Banned terms

streamline, empower, leverage, unlock, elevate, game-changer, revolutionary, AI-powered (unless literally true), hustle, scale your business (as verb).
